Existing goal authorization covers this functional optimization and evaluation. This does not alter role-card tone, rubric thresholds, the seven-call three-role meeting, or its 48-second budget.

Evidence: compact CLI discussions repeatedly finish six role calls but time out in synthesis. Response turns regenerate their initial position, evidence, objection and revision condition even when the judgment is unchanged. The candidate reduces repeated output; actual improvement remains unproven until measured.

Private compact response contract: each of the four content fields is required and accepts its current type or null. Null means retain that same participant's validated initial field verbatim. Empty objection or evidence explicitly clears that field. Missing fields, no matching initial position, or changed=true without any observable content change are rejected. Peer review, source selection, changeReason and changed remain freshly generated and validated. No inherited model-supplied actor/turn identity is accepted.

The server reconstructs the full response turn before the existing public discussion parser. Public persistence, UI, exact peer quotations, objection resolution coverage and source validation remain unchanged. Default reviewed-v25 and position rounds retain their existing full-output schema. The optimization is confined to the server-selected compact candidate.
